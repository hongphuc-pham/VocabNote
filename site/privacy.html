Privacy policy

Applies to Schwa Notes for Android. Last updated 14 September 2026.

Schwa Notes has no account and no analytics. Your words never leave your phone unless you export them. Looking up a word sends only that word to freedictionaryapi.com.

What the app does

  • No account, no sign-in, and no server of ours.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no advertising ID.
  • Schwa Notes reaches the internet only when you ask it to: to look up a word, or to open a link you tapped, in your browser.
  • Your words, IPA, highlights, notes and practice history stay on this phone, and leave it only in a backup you export.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer receives no data from the app: there is no server to send it to, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

Looking up a word

When you tap Look up, the app asks FreeDictionaryAPI.com for that one word. Like any website you visit, their server necessarily sees your IP address and a line naming the app and its version. It does not receive your other words, your notes or anything else from your phone. You can switch look-up off in Settings, and everything else keeps working offline.

Backups

A backup is a file the app makes only when you choose Export backup, and it goes wherever you send it with your phone's share menu. It is never uploaded anywhere by the app. Delete all data in Settings removes everything the app keeps on the phone.

Links you tap

Links such as Open in Cambridge Dictionary, the data-source pages or GitHub open in your browser. What happens there is covered by that website's own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications, asked for only if you turn on the daily practice reminder.
  • Run at start-up, so a reminder you set still arrives after the phone restarts.
  • Vibrate, so the reminder can buzz like any other notification.
  • Internet, used only for look-up and links, as described above.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and loads nothing from any other website. GitHub may keep standard server logs, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If what the app does ever changes, this page changes in the same release, and the date above with it. The history of this page is public in the source repository.
