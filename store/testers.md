# Closed test — recruiting the 12 testers

A personal developer account created after 13 November 2023 must run a **closed test with at
least 12 testers who stay opted in for at least 14 consecutive days** before it can apply for
production (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465).

What counts, and what trips people up:

- Testers must **opt in** through the testing link *and* stay opted in for the whole 14 days. A
  tester who leaves resets nothing for the others, but you need 12 at the same time throughout.
- Recruit **15 or more**. Someone always forgets to accept.
- Testers need a Google account on an Android phone. The email you add must be the one signed in
  to the Play Store on that phone.
- Ask testers to actually open the app a few times. Google asks, when you apply for production,
  what you learned from the test; real use gives you real answers.
- Keep the closed-test build installed; publishing a new build to the same track is fine.

## What to note during the 14 days

Keep a short log in this file (date, who, what they said). The production application asks:

- how you recruited testers,
- how easy it was to get feedback,
- what you changed because of it,
- how you decided the app is ready.
